Privacy Policy — Anagrams

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Anagrams (“the app”) is developed by Tools On Earth LLC (“we,” “us”). We built Anagrams to respect your privacy completely. The short version: we don't collect any data.

Data We Collect

None. Anagrams does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app works entirely offline and has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no networking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game data — settings, statistics, and high scores — is stored only on your device. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

No Tracking, Ads, or Analytics

Anagrams contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Anagrams is a paid app. Your purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment information. Apple may collect data per the Apple Privacy Policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregate sales figures.

Children's Privacy

Anagrams is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data whatsoever from any user, it collects none from children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design — there is simply no data to collect.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
