Anagrams — Family-Friendly

Anagrams is a word game for people of all ages — children, parents, and grandparents alike.

A Curated Word List

Standard word-game dictionaries include many words that aren't suitable for young players. We have carefully reviewed and curated the Anagrams word lists, removing offensive, crude, and mature words so the game is family-friendly and appropriate for all ages.

Definitions are curated the same way: definitions that contain unsuitable language are removed, so learning as you play stays friendly too.

Help Us Keep It Friendly

With hundreds of thousands of words, no review is perfect. If you come across a word or definition you believe is best removed to keep Anagrams friendly for families and all ages, please let us know.

Email your suggestions to support@toolsonearth.org. Every suggestion is reviewed and considered for a future update.
