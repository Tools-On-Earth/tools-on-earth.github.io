Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
You're given a subject word and a time limit. Find as many words as you can from its letters before time runs out. When time's up, you'll see every word you found plus the ones you missed, with definitions.
How do I change the timer?
On the game start screen, choose 1:00, 3:00, or 5:00 minutes.
Can I change the dictionary or word list?
Yes. In Settings, choose the North American or International dictionary, and optionally narrow to a smaller word list.
How do definitions work?
When enabled in Settings, definitions appear for words after each round so you can learn as you play.
How do I reset my statistics?
Open Statistics and use the reset options there.
Do I need an account?
No. Anagrams has no accounts and works entirely offline; your progress stays on your device.
